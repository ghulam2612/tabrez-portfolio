About

Designing rooms people actually want to live in.

I'm an interior designer based in New Delhi, working across residential homes, commercial showrooms, and salons. My process starts with how a space will really be used — then I build the plan, the materials, and the lighting around that.

From space planning and 3D visualization to construction-ready working drawings, I carry every project from first sketch to final handover.
